VIRTUAL VILLAGERS FUN PATCHER

PHYSICAL POPULATION LIMITS

A New Home and The Lost Children have 256 stock villager records.
The Secret City, The Tree of Life, and New Believers have 150 stock records.
Expanded-256 population modes have been removed from the patcher.

1. Double-click "Launch Virtual Villagers Fun Patcher.bat".
2. Choose one of the three population modes:
   - No Population Increase:
     Preserve the stock population cap, progression behavior, and population
     allocation behavior. No population-cap or saturation bytes are changed.
   - Collection Progression Max Pop:
     Collections still raise the cap and are needed to reach the absolute
     maximum. The Secret City also retains its level-3 magic bonus.
   - Immediate Fixed Max Pop:
     The absolute maximum is available immediately. Collections no longer
     affect it, and The Secret City's magic tech no longer affects it.
3. Optional for A New Home: check "School Lessons Grant Skill",
   "Continue Research at Max Technologies", and/or
   "Reenable F6 Clothing Change Cheat", and/or
   "Magic Fruit of Life Alters Mortality" and/or "Builder Action Fixes".
   VV1 Origins is disabled, catalog-hidden, and unavailable. Its legacy Time
   Warp, Cure, Running, doubler, and selected-villager actions are historical
   evidence only and are not emitted. The separate stock-mode command-7 Full
   Mastery candidate remains static-only and runtime/player pending.
4. Optional for The Lost Children: check "Easier Healing Mastery",
   "Teaching Children Grants Skill", "Birth Control",
   "Gong of Wonder Coconuts Fix", and/or "Hospital Recovery Heals".
   VV2 Origins is disabled and catalog-hidden: Time Warp and Food Point Doubler
   were reported to crash after the purchased/success dialog; this does not
   infer whether the charge or action persisted. Legacy Cure, Running, doubler,
   and selected-villager Origins actions are historical evidence only and are
   not emitted. Unrelated VV2 features remain available.
   "Grant Full Mastery to All Villagers" is available as the separate static
   command-7 candidate for stock Collection Progression and Immediate Fixed;
   runtime/player confirmation remains pending.
5. Optional for The Secret City: check
   "Everyone Tries On the Robe" and/or
   "Nature Level 1 Actually Replenishes Food Sources Faster" and/or
   "Nature Level 3 Actually Alters Mortality" and/or
   "Pointing Out a Rare Collectible Always Works" and/or
   "Enable Origins-Exclusive Features". VV3 selected-villager Running and
   Full Heal / Cure All are disabled, catalog-hidden, and absent from GUI, CLI,
   and Select All. Their retained candidate records are evidence only.
6. Optional for The Tree of Life: check
   "Complete Fish Scales = Golden Fish in Nets" and/or "Enable Origins-Exclusive
   Features". VV4 remains the untouched vanilla Breeding and Embracing
   reference; the historical Birth Control candidate is rejected/superseded
   and is not offered or applied.
   VV1 Birth Control also remains disabled and unselectable: exact-build audit
   c8d268d rejected its former food-gate/live-code byte proposal, and no
   replacement hook is certified.
7. Optional for New Believers: check "Heathen Mommy Puzzle Restoration" and/or
   "Easier Devotee Training" and/or
   "Statue Drops: Normal Action or Honoring" and/or
   "VV4 Nursery School Divisor Parity" and/or
   "Enable Origins-Exclusive Features".
8. Optional for any game: check that game's "Write Village Statistics to Text
   File" feature. It refreshes a separate "Village Statistics - Save N.txt"
   file after each successful save of slots 1 through 5.
9. Open "One Game" or "All 5 Games".

PATCH ALL FIVE AT ONCE

1. Use the five separate fields to choose one folder per game. Each folder must
   contain that game's ORIGINAL EXE.
2. Or use "Find All 5 in Parent Folder..." when the EXEs are in the selected
   folder or one folder below it.
3. Optional: use "Modded output location" to choose the parent folder where all
   generated "(Game name) - Modded" folders will be placed. Leave it blank to
   place each generated folder beside its supplied original.
4. Click "Validate All 5", "Dry Run All 5", or "Patch All 5".
5. Use the clickable "Vanilla folder" or "Modified folder" link on any row
   to open the original folder or its separate copied-and-modified folder.
6. After patching, the confirmation window provides clear clickable Vanilla
   Folder and Modded Folder links for all five games.

PATCH ONE GAME

1. Browse to one ORIGINAL game EXE.
2. Optional: use "Modded output location" to choose the parent folder where the
   generated "(Game name) - Modded" folder will be placed. Leave it blank to
   place it beside the supplied original.
3. Click Validate, Dry Run, or Create Modified EXE.
4. Use "Open Vanilla EXE Folder" or "Open Modified EXE Folder" to open the
   selected game's folder in File Explorer.

OPTIONAL PATCH SELECTION

Use "Select All Patches" to turn on every optional fun patch at once. Use
"Deselect All Patches" to turn them all off. These buttons do not change the
selected population mode. Optional patches are grouped under the five
game-title headers, in order: Virtual Villagers - A New Home, The Lost
Children, The Secret City, The Tree of Life, and New Believers. Shared/all-games
patches appear afterward only when present. A dependent checkbox automatically
selects its prerequisite; clearing a prerequisite clears its dependents. The
patcher saves and logs the resolved dependency-first selection. Direct API/CLI
requests that omit a prerequisite are refused before output is written.

OUTPUTS

All three modes and optional-patch combinations use:

(name of game) - Modded\
(name of game) - Modded.exe

Mode and optional-patch details are recorded in the modified EXE's adjacent
.patch-log.json file instead of being added to the EXE or save-folder name.

For each game, the patcher creates one complete copy named
"(Game name) - Modded". The copied folder keeps every original file and
subfolder, including the stock EXE, and adds "(Game name) - Modded.exe" and its
verification log. Applying another population mode refreshes the same short Modded
folder in place after confirmation. If a Modded output location was selected,
these folders are created beneath that chosen parent instead of beside the
original. The patcher does not create temporary
game copies or replacement-backup folders.
Original folders and EXEs are never changed, renamed, replaced, or deleted. The
patcher remembers the selected population mode, output parent, single-game EXE path, and
all five folder paths. Vanilla and Modded links open their respective folders.

SAFE MULTIPLE BIRTHS

All five games normally check the cap before deciding singleton/twins/triplets.
The two population-increase modes prevent overflow at the selected mode's slot ceiling. The No Population Increase mode preserves the stock behavior:
- 3+ spaces: multiples work normally.
- 2 spaces: a rolled triplet becomes twins.
- 1 space: a rolled twin or triplet becomes a singleton.
- 0 spaces: the normal cap check blocks the birth.

Population-adding Island Events are protected in all five games too.
Repeated arrivals stop at the physical ceiling. VV4 and VV5 Abandoned
Infants is reduced below six when fewer than six physical slots remain.
Events that remove villagers are unchanged.

Only the five exact researched stock builds are supported. Unknown, modified,
corrupt, duplicate, or incorrectly assigned EXEs are refused.

VV2 EASIER HEALING MASTERY

When a healer or a villager who prefers Healing has no sick villager to treat,
the patch sends that villager into the game's existing plant-study work state.
That persistent state is used during normal play and time catch-up. Normal
healing, sickness, food, skill gains, and plant availability are unchanged.

VV1 REENABLE F6 CLOTHING CHANGE CHEAT

Press F6 to advance the selected active villager to the next stock outfit. Each
successful outfit change costs exactly 5,000 tech points. With fewer than
5,000 tech points, F6 does nothing and charges nothing. The cycle uses clothing
indices 0 through 19 and wraps from 19 back to 0. Nothing changes if no active
villager is selected. The patch does not change heads,
sex, age, skills, health, jobs, actions, or clothing assets. All non-F6 keys
retain their original behavior.

VV1 SCHOOL LESSONS GRANT SKILL

When an unlocked Going to school action reaches its end, the child gains 7, 8,
or 9 points in one equally random skill: Farming, Building, Research, Healing,
or Parenting. This matches the VV3 Tribal Chief lesson award. Skills remain
capped at 100. An interrupted school action that never reaches its completion
callback gives no skill.

VV1 MAGIC FRUIT OF LIFE ALTERS MORTALITY

Once the Magic Fruit puzzle is completed, its saved puzzle flag shifts every
ordinary villager's mortality curve seven displayed years later. The same stock
mortality routine runs during normal play and time catch-up. Medicine still
adds its original benefit, and the Golden Child keeps the stock exemption.

Whenever a villager finishes Enjoying magic fruit, the final action cleanup
clears sickness and restores health to 100. The fruit remains reusable. An
interrupted action does not cure the villager. The patch stores nothing in
villager names, likes, dislikes, or other record fields.

VV2 TEACHING CHILDREN GRANTS SKILL

When an attending child's full stock Teaching Children lesson queue reaches
its end, that child gains 7, 8, or 9 points in one equally random skill:
Farming, Building, Research, Healing, or Parenting. This matches the VV3
Tribal Chief lesson award. Skills remain capped at 100. An interrupted lesson
that never reaches its completion callback gives no skill. The patch does not
change who teaches, who attends, or how often lessons happen.

VV2 HOSPITAL RECOVERY HEALS

When a villager completes Recovering at the hospital, that villager gains
exactly 1 health point, capped at 100. Stock VV2's hospital action does not
change health. The point is awarded by the final completion callback, so an
interrupted recovery gives no health.

VV2 BIRTH CONTROL

The two writer-reaching opcode-12 candidate scans used by ordinary
autonomous/catch-up pairing and stew recipe 15 reject a candidate whose
internal age is 1000 or greater. The two exact guarded blocks are one optional
feature and are never applied separately. The stock manual carrier/female-only
gate remains unchanged, and no male upper-age gate is added. Direct/event
births, pending delivery, saves, RNG, food, fertility, capacity, messages,
statistics, Love Note, Gong grant, and Silver Mirror clone remain native.

VV2 GONG OF WONDER COCONUTS FIX

The Gong's coconut outcome adds 30 to the coconut trees instead of replacing
their current amount with 30. Both stock outcome paths are corrected. Other
Gong outcomes, coconut consumption, and tree capacity are unchanged.

ALL GAMES WRITE VILLAGE STATISTICS

Enable "Write Village Statistics to Text File" for a game to refresh
"Village Statistics - Save N.txt" after every successful save of slots 1
through 5. The export is per save and written beside that modified game's
executable. It does not depend on a Statistics screen and never changes the
save result if the text export itself fails.

VV3 EVERYONE TRIES ON THE ROBE

Drop one active, living, non-nursing villager on the robe. If the original
callback puts that villager into either the stock failed-fit Trying on the robe
action or the successful Tribal Chief action, every other active, living,
non-nursing villager is sent to the robe area with the stock failed-fit Trying
on the robe action.

The dropped villager keeps the complete normal result and remains the only
villager who can become Tribal Chief. Followers receive the native status,
walk, gestures, and temporary try-on appearance, but never the successful fit,
persistent Chief clothing, or Chief state. Dead, inactive, and nursing
villagers are skipped. Fanout requires the original callback to report a
handled drop and leave the initiator in robe action 120 or 121.

This optional checkbox starts unchecked and supports the two
population-increase modes. Static install, composition, and uninstall checks
pass; player runtime confirmation is still pending. The broadcast feature
itself never reads or writes candidate fields +0xE80 or +0xE88. If the fields
are still zero
before automatic assignment, or no eligible candidate exists, the game's
native failed-fit action 121 is broadcast without granting Chief state.

VV3 NATURE LEVEL 1 ACTUALLY REPLENISHES FOOD SOURCES FASTER

At Nature level 1 or higher, fruit trees become refill-eligible after 2 hours
15 minutes instead of 3 hours, and honey becomes refill-eligible after 45
minutes instead of 1 hour. Fruit trees retain their stock Nature quantity
bonus: approximately 126 instead of 111 fruit for the same stock refill group,
now delivered after the shorter interval. Honey receives the same 42/37
proportional quantity bonus, normalized to its new 45-minute interval. Nature
level 0 keeps stock timing and amounts. The 3,000-unit honey cap is unchanged.

VV3 NATURE LEVEL 3 ACTUALLY ALTERS MORTALITY

At Nature level 3, every ordinary villager's complete mortality curve shifts
seven displayed years later. Medicine's stock longevity threshold is
calculated first, so the two benefits stack. Nature levels 0 through 2 are
unchanged. VV3's shared aging loop applies the shift during normal play and
elapsed-time catch-up.

VV5 HEATHEN MOMMY PUZZLE RESTORATION

The naturally enabled reference creates a 29th, tag-17 Heathen mother and gives
her one nursing baby. The modern new-game initializer omits both, although it
retains the puzzle class, trigger, completion logic, actions, text, and locked
and solved images. This option restores the exact mother-and-baby sequence for
newly created villages and restores the separate 17th-puzzle drawing branch.
The full visible tile shows "This milestone has not been completed!" while
locked and "The Heathen Parent" when completed.
The pair uses two physical population slots. Existing saves are not
retroactively given a new mother.

VV5 EASIER DEVOTEE TRAINING

Any villager with positive Devotion skill can spontaneously choose the game's
original Honoring action. Normally, the autonomous opportunity is confined to
the Retired Chief job state, while other devotees have to be dropped on the
upgradeable statue to begin Honoring. The patch checks actual Devotion skill
instead and keeps the stock idle timing, action queue, and skill-gain behavior.
It does not directly grant skill or change conversion, statue upgrades, manual
assignment, or skill thresholds. Spreading the Word remains a Retired Chief
activity and is not assigned to ordinary devotees.

VV5 STATUE DROPS: NORMAL ACTION OR HONORING

Every applicable statue drop makes a 50/50 choice between Honoring and the
normal action for the statue's current state: Building a statue during
construction, Confused when an upgrade lacks the necessary technology, or
Polishing the Statue for eligible upgradeable and completed statues.
It does not change autonomous work, skill gain amounts, statue upgrades, or
Retired Chief activities.

VV5 ORIGINS FULL MASTERY EXTENSION

The VV5 Full Mastery extension base and Grant Full Mastery candidate are
disabled, catalog-hidden, and unavailable. Their retained records are evidence
only. The Tech/Detail click route remains STOP pending an exact sub_44B560 hook
guard and a click-to-dialog player receipt.

The selected current living Believer receives the native exact-100 Full Mastery
transaction. Cure row/command 5 is withdrawn, unavailable, bypassed, and
unreachable; do not purchase Cure and no 30,000-point Cure behavior is included.
Other VV5 actions and unrelated features remain native.

ORIGINS VILLAGE-WIDE UPGRADES

All five legacy village-wide Origins records remain unavailable. Commands
6/7/8 share one atomic payload, so Running, Full Mastery, and Age 18 remain
fail-closed together until each game receives a complete payload safety GO.
The contained row labels include Grant Full Mastery to All Villagers.
The disabled manifests remain as diagnostic evidence, but their bytes are not
applied. Existing save fields are not cleared, changed, or refunded.

VV3 All Villagers Like Running remains disabled, catalog-hidden, and absent.
Its retained implementation evidence does not authorize publication.
