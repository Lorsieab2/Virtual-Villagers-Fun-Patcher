The Troubling Signs. (requires a Tribal Chief, and 5 other villagers age 18+, and has the same additional requirements as VV2's "A Dangerous Mission" event: a population of 61 or more)

(Name), the tribal chief, has been noticing something troubling. For the past 9 months, (he/she) has heard reports of fish floating dead on the water. Crop harvests have been failing, and birds have been falling out of the sky. Even the villagers have started to feel weak and tired, almost to the point of being unable to work. The scientists and healers have been trying to investigate but have not found a definite explanation for these occurrences.
With so little information to go on, and the problems mounting further, (name) must make a decision quickly for the sake of the village. But, who should (name) go to for advice?

(four choices)

Consult the spiritual leaders:
The spiritual leaders believe that the village should pray and offer gifts. Surely these unfortunate occurrences must stem from transgressions the villagers have made towards the things that guide their fate. “Make haste. The spirits are patient and kind, but such transgressions are not easily forgiven,” (random name) says. “One must be pure of heart, mind and body to right what is wrong. It will take time. Time which grows ever shorter.” Chief (name) thanks the spiritual leaders for their wise counsel and makes (his/her) decree.

Ask the magicians for advice:
The magicians think that the factions are in imbalance, and believe that the magical forces that strengthen Isola have been weakened. “The magic that flows within all things is waning,” (random name) says. “Either our connection, or its source. We must figure out which is true, and remedy it.” Chief (name) thanks the magicians for their wise counsel, and makes (his/her) decree.

Call the naturalists to a discussion:
The naturalists believe that the factions are in imbalance, and that the natural forces that keep Isola healthy have been disrupted. “The natural cycle of rebirth and renewal is fading,” (random name) says. “Soon enough, all of Isola will perish if nothing is done. We must figure out what is destroying it and stop it.” Chief (name) thanks the naturalists for their wise counsel, and makes (his/her) decree.

Talk with the scientists and healers:
The scientists and healers believe that some organic phenomenon is causing the animals and plants to wither and die. “Perhaps they are being poisoned,” (random name) says. “Or, something in their environment is adversely affecting their health. Whatever it is, we must do some in-depth experimentation to get to the bottom of this disaster.” Chief (name) thanks the scientists and healers for their wise counsel, and makes (his/her) decree.

then (the next page, same for every advisor):
“Hear me, O (tribe name)! Your Chief speaks!” (name) calls from on high. A crowd gathers in the amphitheater to listen to (his/her) words.

then, depending on who the Chief asked (the decree page; the effects happen here):

Look amongst yourselves, all who are eligible to work, and from you pick five to represent our tribe. You alone will travel to find the cause of our suffering, and heal it, for the sake of us all. Now go forth, and discuss your plans with my advisors. They will guide you on your sacred mission. May Isola help us all.”
