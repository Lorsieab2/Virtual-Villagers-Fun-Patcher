NEW EVENTS — HEALING, PSYCHOLOGY AND MEDICINE (HEA-01..50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Healers, herbs, teas, poultices, mushrooms, potions, island magic, listening, breathing, routines and rest. Care for the body and care for the mind, with every kind of healing treated with equal respect. Treatments can help, help a little, do nothing or have side effects. Nobody has to be cured, nobody is mocked, and the villager always decides.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.

All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. "Healer" means whoever does the healing in that game; nothing here needs a new job, building or item. No cure is promised anywhere.
