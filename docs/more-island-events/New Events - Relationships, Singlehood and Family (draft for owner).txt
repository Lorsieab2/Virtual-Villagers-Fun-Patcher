NEW EVENTS — RELATIONSHIPS, SINGLEHOOD AND FAMILY (FAM2-01..50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Couples of every kind, courting, quarrels, separations without blame, widowhood and second loves, happily single and childfree villagers, single parents, grandparents, siblings, chosen and extended families, and affection that isn't romantic. Nobody has to love, marry or have children, everyone may say no to affection, and a player's choice never obliges a villager to anything.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Teens appear only with teens of a similar age, and only innocently; children have only innocent crushes; adult intimacy is never shown. Where an event needs a relationship, the casting line asks for one the game records; otherwise everyone is simply two villagers.
