THE ASPIRING ADVENTURER
Revised text, 12 November 2026. A New Home, The Lost Children, The Secret City and The Tree of Life share one version (VV1-VV4). New Believers (VV5) differs only in lost ending 2.

How to read this:
- (he/she), (his/her), (him/her) and (He/She) follow the adventurer's sex.
- (Name) / (name) is the adventurer. In ending A8, (name1) is the adventurer and (name2) is the friend who comes to help.
- In endings A4 and A6, the stranger's (man/woman) and (his/her) follow the stranger's own sex, picked at random.
- (award) is the amount actually given: food for the meat, tech points for the chest.
- Changes from your draft: "their" changed to (his/her) where it means the adventurer; "and (he/she) stuffs" added in the chest find; lost ending 2 says "another tribe" outside New Believers; ending A9 added.


=====================================================================
REQUIREMENTS TO HAPPEN
=====================================================================

The event can happen only when at least TWO ELIGIBLE villagers are in the village: two who could each be picked by the rules in "Who is never picked" below. With fewer than two, it cannot happen.

When it can happen, it takes an equal share of the island-event draw, like the game's own events. It uses the game's usual timing between island events. Pick Island Event can also choose it, under the same requirement.

Exception: Custom Island Events (Story / Cheat Upgrades). There the player decides, so this requirement does not apply.


=====================================================================
WHO IS NEVER PICKED (the adventurer, or the friend in ending A8)
=====================================================================

In every game:
- anyone under 14 (children and babies);
- anyone nursing a baby, mother or father;
- anyone dead (a body or grave), or not in the village.

And in each game:
- A New Home: the Golden Child.
- The Lost Children: the Esteemed Elders' totem statues.
- The Secret City: the tribal chief.
- The Tree of Life: no one else.
- New Believers: every Heathen (believers only), the chief, the Retired Chief and the other special roles the game itself never sends on events, and Reanimated spirits.

The friend in ending A8 is never the adventurer. Because the event needs two villagers who can be picked, there is always a friend, so A8 can always happen. (Only a Custom Island Event, which skips that requirement, could leave no one to be the friend; then A8 becomes A5.)
With fewer than two villagers who can be picked, the event does not happen (except through Custom Island Events).

HOW IT PLAYS (not shown in the game)
=====================================================================

Who: one villager aged 14 or older (never anyone under 14; the event needs at least two who can be picked), never a villager nursing a baby (mother or father), never the Golden Child, The Secret City's tribal chief or any other special villager; in New Believers, believers only. The friend in ending A8 follows the same rules.

Choice A:
- Find: nothing 50%, meat 25% (food), artifacts 25% (tech points).
- Ending: half the time a surviving ending, half the time a deadly one.
  - Surviving with a find: A1, A5 or A8 (A8 is always possible: the event needs two villagers who can be picked).
  - Surviving after finding nothing: A7 or A9.
  - Deadly: A2 or A4 (dies, gets a grave), A3 or A6 (disappears, no grave).
  - The reward is given only in A1, A5 and A8.

Choice B:
- Home safe 90%, lost 10%.
- Lost endings 1 to 6, each 1 in 6:
  - 1: brought to the healers, then one of three, each 1 in 3:
    - dies, gets a grave;
    - full recovery;
    - health left at 10-50% and now dislikes running.
  - 2: New Believers: becomes a Heathen. Other games: gone for good, no grave.
  - 3: gains 20 to 30 points (at random) in Farming, Building, Research or Healing (one at random).
  - 4 and 5: dies, gets a grave.
  - 6: disappears, no grave.

Deaths count as "Unknown causes". The reward follows "Island Events Scale With Population" or "...With Stored Food and Tech Points" when either is ticked.

REWARDS (the owner, 2026-11-12: "a random tech and food point award, scaling"):
The amount is random, in the range that game's own island events give, then scaled with the village like the game's other island events.
  A New Home ........... food 200 to 1,500    tech points 500 to 4,999
  The Lost Children .... food 100 to 1,500    tech points 500 to 3,000
  The Secret City ...... food 200 to 600      tech points 1,000 to 2,000
  The Tree of Life ..... food 300             tech points 500 to 5,000
  New Believers ........ food 50 to 300       tech points 500 to 3,000
