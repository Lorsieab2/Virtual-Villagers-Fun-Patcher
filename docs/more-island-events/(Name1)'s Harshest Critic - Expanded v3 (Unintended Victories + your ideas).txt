[owner] DEVOTION SUPPORT (Lorsieab2's rule, replaces the line that was here): in VV1-VV4, which have no Devotion skill, a Devotion task's skill reward goes to one of the other five skills (Farming, Building, Research, Healing, Parenting), chosen at random among those that make sense for the task (e.g. tending the sacred garden = Farming, raising a shrine = Building, reading omens = Research, a healing rite = Healing, teaching the young the old prayers = Parenting), or is flavor only. In VV5 the reward can be Devotion or one of those five, at random where it makes sense in the event.
[owner] CRAFTS COUNT AS THEIR MAIN SKILL (Lorsieab2's rule): a craft or job counts as the main profession it grows out of (cooking, fishing, hunting = Farming; carving, pottery, weaving, jewelry, weaponsmithing, bow-making = Building; stargazing, scrolls and books, potions, mapping = Research; herbalism, bonesetting = Healing; teaching the young = Parenting; prayer, ritual, omens = Devotion in VV5). A craft that fits more than one main profession (archery = hunting and bow-making; a medicine potion = Research and Healing) can give skill in each of them, or in one of them at random. If it doesn't make sense, it's flavor.
[owner] Potions, stews, remedies, poultices, salves, tonics and anything similar = Research OR Healing. Magic, spells, charms, blessings and the like = Healing OR Devotion (VV1-VV4, which have no Devotion: Healing).
