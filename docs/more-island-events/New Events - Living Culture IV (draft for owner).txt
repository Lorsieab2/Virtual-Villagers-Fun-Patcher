NEW EVENTS - LIVING CULTURE IV: ROMANCE, COURTSHIP AND WEDDINGS (LCD-01..50, draft, 2026-11-18)
Crushes and courting on Isola: wondering whether someone likes you back, flowers and love songs, first dates, serenades gone wrong, proposals, weddings big and small, how many children, breakups and starting over.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. The owner waived the review ("I'm sure the draft events are fine"); every event passed the Isola rules and the final 15-point review, logged in the notes.
(name1) to (name4) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager. Couples may be any two people.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Teens (14-17) court only each other and never beyond romance; marriage and anything more is 18 and over; adult couples are at most 15 years apart. A [Solidarity: raise/lower/neutral] tag marks how an ending feels for the village (a planned counter; no effect yet).
