NEW EVENTS — TINY ISLAND MOMENTS, T126 to T175 (draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Fifty more tiny moments continuing T001-T125 (your 500 Tiny Island Moments, played as 125 openings with 4 endings each).
Same shape as your answer to Question 1 (Option A): each moment is one opening sentence plus four alternative one-sentence endings, and the game picks one ending at random. One short, natural title per moment, as in your Question 2 answer (Option A). No choices.
All new topics: none of these openings repeats a T001-T125 topic (lanterns, leaf boats, rain, cooking, potions, nets, holidays, gifts, promises, teaching elders, parents, siblings, snakes, pebbles, ropes, races, parchment, friendship, workdays, memories, gardens, fishing, music, rest, sharing).
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names. All five games. No new mechanics, sprites or buildings. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
