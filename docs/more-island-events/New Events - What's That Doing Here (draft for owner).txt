NEW EVENTS — WHAT'S THAT DOING HERE? (WD-01 to WD-50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Something out of place turns up: an animal, a plant or an object where it has no business being. Villagers react: amused, curious, puzzled. No explanation is required, and most of the time none is found.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Everything here is a passing moment: nothing new appears in the game world, no animal or object is added, and nothing becomes a permanent trait. Animals are never harmed; where one needs to go home, it goes home.

WD-01. What's a Penguin Doing on the Beach?
(picks an adult)

(name1) is walking along the beach at dawn when (he/she) sees a small black-and-white bird standing at the water's edge. It is upright, like a tiny person. It has very short wings. It is, as far as (name1) can tell, wearing a tiny formal outfit. It looks at (name1). (name1) looks at it. Neither of them has any idea what the other one is.

Fetch (name2) to See:
(name2) comes running, takes one look, and sits down on the sand. "What IS it?" The bird waddles a few steps toward them, stops, and makes a noise like a creaky door. "I think it's lost," says (name1). "I think it's from somewhere a lot colder than here." They watch it for an hour. Then it turns, waddles into the waves, and swims away faster than any bird has a right to. Nobody believes them at dinner.

Keep a Respectful Distance:
(name1) sits down a little way off and keeps very still. The bird preens itself, looks out to sea, and seems to think hard about something. (name1) wonders where it came from, and how far it swam, and whether its family is wondering where it went. After a while it slides into the water and is gone. (name1) never sees another one. (He/she) never forgets it, either.

(name1) tiptoes upstream and fills (his/her) jar at a different spot. When (he/she) comes back an hour later, the seal has gone, leaving a large seal-shaped dent in the mud. (name1) shows it to the children. By the evening the story has grown, and the seal was apparently as big as a canoe and could talk.

NOTES FOR THE OWNER
- Effects are kept small and marked in [brackets]: [small food gain] (WD-07, WD-33, WD-37). Everything else is story only.
- WD-32 and WD-39 are single popups with no choice.
- Every animal that turns up is a real animal (penguin, seal, pelican, frog, bat, owl, heron, pig, lizard, chicken, duck, seabirds, moth, cricket, ants, seahorse, reef fish, butterfly, crab, shrimp). No legendary creature is named or implied. None is harmed; each one goes home or is left in peace.
