NAT-01. The Crab in the Blue Cup
(picks a child aged 5-13)

(name1) has found a hermit crab on the beach, and it isn't living in a shell. It considers. Then it climbs back into the blue cup and walks off. (name1) is outraged. (name2), who watched the whole thing, says, "Well, it's a very nice cup." Leave It Be:
(name1) decides it's the crab's business where it lives. For days afterward (he/she) keeps spotting it on the beach, the only blue thing for miles, bonking along. (He/she) names it Cup. Nobody can tell where cups like that come from, or why one ended up here. (name1) has decided it doesn't matter. Cup is home.

"And you walked uphill both ways to catch them," says (name2). (name1) laughs. "I'm not saying you're doing it wrong. I'm saying the sea's different." They argue about it pleasantly over dinner.
