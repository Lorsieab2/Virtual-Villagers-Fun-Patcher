NEW EVENTS — TALL TALES AND SELF-INTEREST (TT-01 to TT-50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Boasting, stretched stories, little vanities, wanting the credit, the best seat or the bigger half. Most of it is harmless and funny; kind people can be boastful too. Nobody is exposed for the sake of it, and nobody needs a lesson.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Everything here is a passing moment: nothing new appears in the game world and nothing becomes a permanent trait.

NOTES FOR THE OWNER
- Effects are kept small and marked in [brackets]: only TT-40 has one ([small food gain]). Everything else is story only.
- No single popups in this set; every event has two choices.
- Most of these are harmless boasting, stretched stories or comfortable self-interest, and they end without anyone being exposed or punished. Where someone loses out a little (TT-15 the shared mango, TT-19 the crispy bits, TT-26 the shell trade, TT-35 the bigger half), it's small and nobody is shamed.
- TT-10 (entering the wild melon) is the one where the boast crosses into something unfair. It's left open on purpose: (name2) notices and says nothing, and what (name1) does next is (name1)'s business. No forced confession.
- TT-16 (the stubbed toe): the toe really did hurt; the villager just keeps enjoying the attention a little longer. Written so that it never suggests anyone fakes pain or illness.
- TT-24 and TT-28 (elders' old feats) keep the elders' dignity: each chooses to try, and each ends by teaching or passing it on.
- TT-29 was reworked so the "improvements" are a heroic pose, a fish and a seabird, never the villager's face or body.
- TT-36: the faraway lands are openly made up; no real outside place is named or described.
- TT-50: the bamboo water channel is story only; nothing new appears in the game.
- Avoided premises already in the catalog or drafts: the fisher's exaggerated catch (ODD-06), credit taken for someone else's discovery (The Stolen Discovery, The Missing Credit, The Rival Coworkers), the exaggerated courtship story, the child's embellished retelling, the Great Crab Story, the villager who invented sleeping, the weather dance, Looking Busy and the Great Chore Trade, Who Taught Whom to Swim, and the rulebook's own examples (the enormous fish, the modest scientist, the famous ancestor who outran a storm). TT-18 (the best shade) is the rulebook's "convenient chore" idea in a new setting; drop it if you'd rather keep that example for yourself.
