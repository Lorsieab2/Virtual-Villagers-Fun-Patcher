HEATHEN-ONLY EVENTS (HE-01 to HE-45, draft for the owner, 2026-11-23, target v1.35.98)
New Believers only. Island events about the Heathens' own lives: honoring their dead, Heathen versions of events our villagers already have, and small moments of camp life.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2)/(name3) fill in with the cast villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her), (himself/herself) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply (the 13 sensitivity rules, the 28 hard rules, the scope answers, the style guide, Stop the Overediting). Event titles and choices are in Title Case; event text is in sentence case.

WHO IS CAST
- "a Heathen" = any Heathen EXCEPT the six special Heathens (the Heathen Doctor, the Heathen Chief and the Retired Chief, the Master Scientist, the Master Builder, the Master Farmer and the Heathen Mommy, roles 12-17). Special Heathens are never cast and nothing in these events ever touches them. Also never a Heathen who is sick or at 0 health (the game's own Heathen picker rules).
- Only Heathens are cast unless an event says otherwise. No believer is cast in sections A and C. Some events in section B cast a believer too; they say so.
- Every event needs its Heathens to be there: if too few Heathens qualify, the event cannot happen. When every Heathen has joined the tribe, none of these events can happen any more.

THE OWNER'S RULES APPLIED HERE (2026-11-23)
- "Everything that happens to our villagers happens to heathens too." Personal outcomes (skills, looks, likes and dislikes, health, injury, moods, relationships) apply to the cast Heathens exactly as they would to villagers.
- "Flavor only = things that raise/lower tech, damage or build structures etc when only heathens are involved." Food, tech points, structures, puzzles, research, collectibles and other tribe resources are STORY TEXT ONLY in these Heathen-only events: shown in the words, never changed in the game. Such a line says it happens to the Heathens' village and ends with "(no effect)": "The Heathens' village gains food. (no effect)" (Guardians of Isola: "The Isolans' village gains food. (no effect)").
- Owner's global rule (2026-11-23): ANY outcome that is flavor only, or changes nothing in the game, ends with "(no effect)". Every such outcome below does.
- Statistics never change because of these events. Heathens are never counted in any Village Statistics row.
- No event here kills anyone, makes anyone disappear, or makes anyone join or leave the tribe. Nobody is pressured toward the tribe's faith; the Heathens' own ways are shown with the same respect as the villagers' (no villain framing, no "lesser" framing, no conversion as a reward). Their dead are mentioned plainly where the text needs it, never graphically, never with a silver lining.

GUARDIANS OF ISOLA REWRITE (every event below)
With "Guardians of Isola Rewrite" installed, every text below (title, page, choice, Pick Island Event line, log line) reads with the owner's pairs: Heathen -> Isolan, Heathens -> Isolans, Heathens' -> Isolans', a Heathen -> an Isolan, "the tribe" (meaning the believers, as against the Heathens) -> "the tribe" (unchanged), "believer(s)" -> "tribe member(s)" (faction sense). Where a title holds the word, its Guardians title is written out under the event. Nothing calls anyone "the Guardians".

EFFECT TAGS
[works today]  = the engine already lets a Heathen's role take this effect (an action, a health change of -30 or better, the sick mark, a story trait).
[needs E2]     = a personal effect the engine refuses for Heathens today (skills, likes, dislikes, looks, larger health changes). Needs engine change E2 (end of this file). Until then it would be flavor.
[needs E9]     = needs the encampment honoring/reflecting actions (engine change E9).
[needs E8]     = needs a requirement the engine lacks today (at least one grave; Heathens present).
[needs E10]    = needs a group effect over the camp's Heathens (engine change E10).
Odds: every event takes an equal share of the island-event draw like the other added events (the owner's 30-50% replacement rule, population gate only for deadly events, and none of these is deadly). Outcome odds are given per event; "equal" means each listed outcome is equally likely.


################################################################################
A. THE HEATHENS HONOR THEIR DEAD (HE-01 to HE-13)
################################################################################
The game never makes a Heathen grave, and Heathens do not die in ordinary play, so "their dead" in these events are people the Heathens lost before or long ago: elders who passed, people lost in the years when the two peoples were at odds (implied only), a child lost long ago, a friend who joined the tribe and now rests in the mausoleum. The mausoleum events need at least one grave there [needs E8]. The encampment events send the Heathen to the camp's mud hut [needs E9]; without it the Heathen just stops and sits (Sitting) [works today].

(picks a Heathen adult, 18 or older)
Requirement: at least one Heathen. Odds: equal.
