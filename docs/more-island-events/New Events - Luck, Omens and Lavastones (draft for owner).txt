NEW EVENTS — LUCK, OMENS AND LAVASTONES (LO-01 to LO-50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
LO-01 to LO-30: superstitions, omens, coincidences, good luck and bad luck. Nothing is ever confirmed or debunked; luck is never a punishment or a reward; believers and skeptics are both respected.
LO-31 to LO-50: lavastones, crystallized sources of power, like gemstones, that carry immense power. They can help, harm or do something in between: save a life, make someone ill, change someone for good, or very rarely kill. Their power may work quietly over time; a stone may crack, go cold or be lost. Where lavastones come from is never explained. Some villagers think they are sacred, some think they are spiritual, some think they are simply a strange kind of rock; all of those views are respected, and none is settled.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Lavastones are story only: no stone object, sprite or inventory item is added to the game. Effects use only existing effects. Where a choice has several possible outcomes, the game picks one at random with the odds shown, and the odds are never a judgment on the villager.

LO-34 and LO-50 are built around that ambiguity.
