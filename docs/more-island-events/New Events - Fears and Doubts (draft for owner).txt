NEW EVENTS — FEARS AND DOUBTS (FEA-01..50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Fears big and small, sensible and not, self-doubt, feeling like a fraud, dread with no reason, wanting and fearing the same thing, and courage that is still frightened. Nobody has to be brave, nobody is mocked, and a fear does not have to be conquered by the end.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.

All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Everything here is a passing moment: no fear becomes a lasting dislike or trait unless an effect says so, and none do.
