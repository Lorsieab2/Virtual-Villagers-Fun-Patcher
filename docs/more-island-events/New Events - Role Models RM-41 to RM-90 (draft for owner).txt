NEW EVENTS — ROLE MODELS AND GOOD EXAMPLES, RM-41 to RM-90 (draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Fifty more events continuing your "Role Models and Good Examples — 40 Events". Same shape as yours: a SETUP, choices A-D (a few have three), and two possible outcomes per choice; the game picks one outcome from the chosen choice.
Being a good example never guarantees a good result here: outcomes include successes, compromises, delays, polite refusals and things left unresolved.
Written to the Isola rules. All five games unless noted. No new mechanics. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.

EVENT RM-41 — THE SLOW SPRING
SETUP: During a dry spell, the spring above the village slows to a trickle, and a long line of villagers with water jars forms before dawn.
A. Fill Small Portions in Turn.
A1. Everyone leaves with a little water, and nobody walks home with an empty jar.
A2. The first few households are satisfied, but the line moves so slowly that several people give up and return at midday.
B. Arrange Turns Through the Night.
B1. Households take turns filling jars after dark, and the morning line shrinks to almost nothing.
B2. The night turns are tiring, and the group agrees to try them only until the rain returns.
C. Ask Who Needs Water Most Urgently.
C1. A family with a feverish elder is filled first, and nobody in line objects.
C2. Several households say their need is urgent, and the villagers decide to share equally after all.
D. Clear the Spring Before Filling.
D1. Two villagers remove fallen leaves and silt, and the trickle runs a little stronger.
D2. The clearing makes no difference, but the workers learn where the water truly rises.

B2. Several village chickens reach the scraps first, and the dog waits for another evening.
C. Ask a Healer to Look at the Limp.

C2. The dog will not let anyone near, and the healer recommends patience over force.
D. Let the Children Help Make It a Sleeping Place.
D1. The children line an old basket with leaves, and the dog uses it the very first night.
D2. The dog ignores the basket entirely and sleeps under a canoe instead, to the children’s amusement.
