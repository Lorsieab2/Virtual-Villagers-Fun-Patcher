ISOLA — FATHERS, PREGNANCY & PARENTHOOD
30 ORIGINAL READY-MADE NARRATIVE EVENT DRAFTS
Version: 2026-11-15

FORMAT: Each event has a setup, 4 player choices, and 2 alternative narrative outcomes per choice.
SCOPE: Narrative drafts only. No changes to natural conception, sex flags, pregnancy eligibility, or UI status are implied.
CASTING: A pregnant male character must be explicitly established as a trans man who can carry a pregnancy; do not infer that all male villagers can conceive.
A Custom Island Event may be used where the existing engine requires player-selected parenthood. These drafts do not assert that ready-made events currently support male pregnancy.
Respect character agency. Outcomes are alternatives, not sequential consequences.

EVENT 01: The Father Who Carries
THEME: Trans man / pregnancy
CAST / CONDITIONS: Adult trans man capable of carrying; pregnancy already established; optional partner

After several mornings of nausea, a father learns from the healer that he is expecting a child. He sits beside the hospital, considering what he wants and whom he trusts with the news.

CHOICES AND POSSIBLE OUTCOMES:
  A. Tell a trusted partner
     Outcome 1: They listen, ask what he wants, and make plans together.
     Outcome 2: The news unsettles them; they agree to talk again after thinking.
  B. Ask the healer about his options
     Outcome 1: He receives practical information without pressure to decide immediately.
     Outcome 2: He leaves with more questions and arranges another visit.
  C. Take time alone
     Outcome 1: A quiet walk helps him understand his own feelings.
     Outcome 2: The uncertainty weighs on him, and he later seeks company.
  D. Confide in a friend
     Outcome 1: His friend offers support without assuming how he feels.
     Outcome 2: His friend blurts out an assumption, then apologizes and listens.
