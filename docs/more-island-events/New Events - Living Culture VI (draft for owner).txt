NEW EVENTS - LIVING CULTURE VI: LAVASTONES AND THE SCHOOLS OF THOUGHT (LCF-01..50, draft, 2026-11-18)
Lavastones, the glowing orange-pink stones of Isola's lore, found on beaches and falling from the sky, and what they cost; and the island's schools of thought, Nature, Science, Spiritualism and Magic, arguing with and borrowing from each other.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. The owner waived the review ("I'm sure the draft events are fine"); every event passed the Isola rules and the final 15-point review, logged in the notes.
(name1) to (name4) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Lavastones exist only in story: where they come from and what magic is are never settled, and no school of thought is simply right. A [Solidarity: raise/lower/neutral] tag marks how an ending feels for the village (a planned counter; no effect yet).
