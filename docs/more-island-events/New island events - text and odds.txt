THE CAVE EXPLORERS and THE CLIMB
Part of the "More Island Events" patch (v1.35.97). Your text, unaltered, apart from fixing two broken name placeholders and the one edit you approved.
(name) is always the villager's full name.

REWARDS (both events, and The Aspiring Adventurer): a random amount in the range that game's own island events give, then scaled with the village like that game's other island events.
  A normal reward: A New Home food 200 to 1,500, tech points 500 to 4,999; The Lost Children tech points 500 to 3,000.
  A large reward is twice a normal one; a huge reward is three times a normal one.

THE CLIMB (The Lost Children)
==========================================================================

REQUIREMENTS TO HAPPEN
- There is at least one eligible villager: 18 or older, and at least a Trainee in Research (20 points or more).
- Custom Island Events can queue it anyway, as long as such a villager exists.

WHO IS NEVER PICKED
- Anyone under 18.
- Anyone below 20 Research.
- A nursing mother or father.
- Special villagers.

The same villager is (name) throughout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
First popup (the opening). The player chooses "Go with the plan" or "Follow the scientists' advice".

(name) is known for their crazy, spontaneous, yet sometimes genius ideas. (he/she) is an avid researcher and has been studying the tribe’s history for a long time. (he/she) believes that two of the tribe’s founders fell down the Great Falls, but nothing definitive is known about their origins. (name) proposes trying to retrace their steps by climbing the waterfall and offers to devise a plan. The others are skeptical about (his/her) claims and warn (him/her) not to take matters into (his/her) own hands without further proof. (name) knows that the plan could be dangerous, but what if (he/she) is right? Should (he/she) go through with (his/her) plan, or follow the other researchers’ advice?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Go with the plan. Then ONE ending, 50% each.

(Name) revises (his/her) plan until (he/she) believes it is practically foolproof. At dawn the next morning, (he/she) sneaks out of the hut before anyone else has woken up and makes their way toward the waterfall. Setting up (his/her) climbing gear, (he/she) makes the perilous ascent up the falls.

C1 -- THE FALL: DIES. Chance 50%. A grave, “Unknown causes”.
For several hours, (he/she) carefully scales the jagged rocks until (he/she) can almost see over the edge of the falls. Just a little further… Oops! (name)’s hand loses its grip and (he/she) falls many dozens of meters to the hard, unforgiving ground. When the villagers wake up, they discover (name)’s body and weep bitterly for their fallen colleague. (He/she) will be remembered as a passionate dreamer that dared to do the impossible.

C2 -- THE SUMMIT: SUCCEEDS. Chance 50%. Research becomes fully mastered, the village gains a large tech reward (2 times normal), and every villager celebrates.
For several hours, (he/she) carefully scales the jagged rocks until (he/she) can almost see over the edge of the falls. Just a little further… With one last stretch, (name) climbs onto the final rock and stands in victory. From this height, (he/she) can see over the entire village and jungle canopy. (He/she) thinks (he/she) can even see other settlements from up here! Behind (him/her), (he/she) hears the rushing of the falls echoing through a large cave mouth leading to somewhere unknown. Checking (his/her) notes and doing some quick calculations, (he/she) is almost certain the cave tunnel must lead somewhere close to the Southern Shore of Isola. (Name) fervently writes down (his/her) deductions and makes a detailed sketch of the area before making the careful descent back home. That day, (he/she) presents (his/her) findings to the others, who work together to perfect (his/her) plan. Over several months they send an expedition team to the perilous peak, and confirm that (name) was right about everything. They name (name) a Distinguished Scientist, and recognize (his/her) achievements in a grand ceremony. (name) is certainly setting a name for (himself/herself) throughout the entire scientific community! Your village gains (award) tech points.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow the scientists' advice -- GAINS 10 to 20 RESEARCH (at random). Nothing else happens.

(name) admits that (his/her) plan still needs some refining. (He/she) goes back to the drawing board to double-check it. After scrutinizing it thoroughly, even (he/she) sees glaring flaws in its execution, and writes a report detailing all (his/her) mistakes. The next day (he/she) consults (his/her) senior colleagues to help (him/her) work out the kinks in (his/her) plan, and over time (name) learns many things from their strict guidance. It turns out (he/she) had much to learn, but was too blinded by ambition to notice their shortcomings. (Name) promises to be more humble and learn from (his/her) elders before stubbornly taking matters into (his/her) own hands.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Go with the plan:
    dies in the fall (C1) .................. 50%
    reaches the top (C2) ................... 50%
  Follow the scientists' advice: always +10 to 20 Research.

(Name) has been swimming underwater as a hobby. One day, (he/she) spots a pod of dolphins playing just offshore! They seem to be chasing something, but (name) is unsure whether (he/she) can hold (his/her) breath long enough to see what. What should (he/she) do?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Swim towards the dolphins" (then A or B, 50% each):
(name) swims towards the dolphins, being careful not to disturb them.

A -- THE HUNT. Chance 50%. FARMING SET TO 100 (fully mastered), and a LARGE food reward (2 times normal):
(He/she) gleefully watches the clever dolphins herd shoals of fish through giant bubble rings, disorienting them enough to become easy prey. “What magnificent creatures!” (he/she) thinks.
Later, (he/she) shares (his/her) observations with the researchers and farmers, which revolutionize their catches that season. (name) becomes a skilled fisherman and is lauded as a pioneer of aquaculture. Your villagers gain (award) food.

B -- THE TAIL SLAP. Chance 50%. LOSES 30 to 50% HEALTH (at random; never dies from it):
Ouch! (name) is blinded by the slap of a large fluke in (his/her) face. (He/she) must have gotten too close to one of them and accidentally gotten hit by their tail flipper. (name) holds (his/her) nose to prevent any more red coming out. Luckily, (name) is not too far from the surface, but if (he/she) had been any deeper… (he/she) shudders to think about what might happen. (He/she) surfaces, leaves the beach, and heads off to the doctors for some medical treatment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Hang back" -- a SMALL food reward (half a normal amount):
(name) doubts that it’s a good idea to follow the dolphins. They clearly seem to be busy doing something important, and (he/she) is second-guessing whether they’re playing anyway. (name) resolves to stick to the shallows and gather shellfish and seaweed on the nearby rocks. Your village gains (award) food.
