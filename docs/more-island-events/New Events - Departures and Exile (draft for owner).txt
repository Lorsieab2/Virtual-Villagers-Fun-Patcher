NEW EVENTS — DEPARTURES AND EXILE (DEP-01..50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Leaving and being left: moving huts, short journeys, visitors going home, goodbyes done well and badly, homesickness, and the rare person who sails away for good. Choosing to leave is never a rejection of one's people, and choosing to stay is never a failure.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
