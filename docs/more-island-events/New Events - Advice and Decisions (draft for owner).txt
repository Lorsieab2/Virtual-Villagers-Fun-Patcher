NEW EVENTS — ADVICE AND DECISIONS (ADV-01..50, draft for the owner, 2026-11-15)
Asking for advice, giving it, ignoring it, following half of it, and deciding alone. Advice can be wrong, even from elders and experts. Following it doesn't guarantee a good outcome and ignoring it doesn't cause a bad one. Feeling heard with no decision is a perfectly good ending, and nobody ever has to admit someone else was right.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. Edit anything and send it back; nothing ships until you approve.
(name1)/(name2) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. No outcome here is a punishment for taking or ignoring advice.
