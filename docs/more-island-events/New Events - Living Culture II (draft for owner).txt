NEW EVENTS - LIVING CULTURE II: THE LAND, MEMORY AND NEW TRADITIONS (LCB-01..50, draft, 2026-11-18)
Isola's ceremonies for the land and sea, its ways of remembering and mourning, and the new traditions its people make, question and change: rain songs, reef promises, memory gardens, funerals, forgotten melodies and festivals nobody planned.
Written in the style of the owner's own events. The owner waived the review ("I'm sure the draft events are fine"); every event passed the Isola rules and the final 15-point review, logged in the notes.
(name1) to (name4) fill in with villagers' names; (he/she), (him/her), (his/her) follow the villager.
All Isola rules apply. All five games unless noted. Isola's traditions are its own: no real people is named and no sacred chant or rite is copied; real-world inspirations are only in the notes. Ancestors are remembered, not worshipped. A [Solidarity: raise/lower/neutral] tag marks how an ending feels for the village (a planned counter; no effect yet).

LCB-01. The Song of the First Rain
(picks an elder and a child)

It has not rained for forty days. The gardens are brown and the stream is a trickle. Then, this afternoon, the sky goes dark and the first fat drops hit the dust. (name2) is already at the door, bouncing. (name1) knows the rain song by heart, but (he/she) also knows how badly the village needs every drop. What should they do first?

Dance in the Rain:

(name1) and (name2) run out into the rain, and the whole village follows. They sing the rain song with their faces turned up and their mouths open. Some of the water pots are left out by accident and fill up anyway. (name2) slips in the mud four times and declares it the best day of (his/her) life. [effect: everyone celebrates] [Solidarity: raise]

Catch Every Drop First:

(name1) sends (name2) running from hut to hut, shouting for everyone to set out pots, bowls and anything that holds water. By the time every pot is out, the rain is easing. They sing the rain song anyway, a bit damp and a bit late. The village has water for weeks. [effect: small food gain] [Solidarity: neutral]

LCB-23. The Festival Nobody Planned
(picks two adults)

LCB-24. The Two Villages' Chorus
