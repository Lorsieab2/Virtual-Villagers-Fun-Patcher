VIRTUAL VILLAGERS - HIDDEN GAME RULES
=====================================
All five games: A New Home, The Lost Children, The Secret City, The Tree of Life, New Believers
Written 2026-10-01 from the stock (unpatched) game executables, the Fun Patcher's research notes
and your saves.


INTRODUCTION
------------
You found out that A New Home shows a hut's scaffold at 15 villagers, but builders won't touch it
until there are 23, and they walk away from it if the village shrinks. The game never tells you.
This file collects every rule of that kind we could find: hidden gates, thresholds and dice rolls
that quietly change what villagers do.

How to read it
  - Each rule has a plain title, then:
      What     what the game does, with the exact numbers
      Tip      what it means for you as a player
      Patch    whether a Fun Patcher patch changes it ("not changed by the patcher" if none)
      Tag      the evidence (see below)
  - Ages. The games count age in steps of 1/20 of a year. At Normal speed one step is 6 real
    minutes (3 at Fast, 10 at Slow), so one game year is 2 real hours at Normal.
  - "Medicine level" means the Medicine technology. It starts at 1 in every game, and the maximum
    is 3.
  - "A roll of 25 or less out of 100" means 26 chances in 100, and so on.

Evidence tags
  [code]            traced in the stock game executable for this report
  [code, re-traced] one of the facts you asked to have checked again; traced again for this report
  [save]            also confirmed in your own save files
  [observed live]   seen in the running game during earlier live tests
  [repo research]   taken from the patcher's earlier research notes; not traced again this time
  [unverified]      could not be confirmed; treat it as a lead, not a fact
No game was run for this report, so apart from the [save] and [observed live] items, everything here
comes from reading the program, not from watching it play.

Patch status
  - "v1.35.44" means the population-hut change now on main (merged, not yet tagged as a release).
  - "Catch-up update" means the work on the fix/vv2-healers-study-catch-up branch, which is not
    merged yet. It gives the catch-up boost of Builders and Healers Work First, continues The Lost
    Children's plant study during catch-up, and installs the companions before the first catch-up.

Code that exists in a game but never runs is not listed as a rule. It is collected at the end under
"Looks like a rule but never runs".

RULES SHARED BY ALL FIVE GAMES
======================================================================================

TIME AWAY (catch-up: what happens while the game is closed, and Time Warp)

S1. Time away counts oddly: more than a day away gives more progress than half a day
  What  Each villager carries a "time owed" total, checked when you load:
        - Owed time over 24 hours is cut to exactly 24 hours.
        - Otherwise, owed time over a speed limit is cut to about 8.6 hours (31,000 seconds).
          The limit is 8.6 hours at Normal, 10.6 hours at Fast and 6.6 hours at Slow.
        Results:
        - At Normal, 8.6 hours away counts in full, 9 to 24 hours away counts as only 8.6, and
          anything over 24 hours counts as a full 24.
        - At Slow, 7 to 24 hours away is rounded UP to 8.6 hours.
        - 8.6 hours is about 4.3 game years at Normal, 2.6 at Slow and 8.6 at Fast.
          24 hours is about 12 / 7.2 / 24 game years.
  Tip   A short absence (under 8 hours) or a long one (over a day) gives full value. An absence of
        10 to 23 hours loses the time past 8.6 hours.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code, re-traced] The same 24-hour and 31,000-second limits are in all five games.

S2. Only healthy, idle adults do anything while you are away
  What  During catch-up a villager acts only if they are 14 or older, not sick, have health of 20
        or more, and are not pregnant, nursing or busy. Each one gets 5 work turns per age step in
        A New Home and 4 in the other games, and only while more than 2 age steps behind.
        Children never act.
  Tip   No lessons, no child play and no child mushroom-finding happen while the game is closed.
        Sick or weak adults also sit out.
  Patch Builders and Healers Work First (catch-up update) makes builders and healers try their
        own job first about 3 times in 4 during catch-up. Otherwise not changed.
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

S3. While you are away, a "Parenting" turn becomes a pairing only 6% of the time
  What  If the catch-up job picker chooses Parenting, the villager tries to pair only on a roll of
        5 or less out of 100 (6 in 100). The other turns are used for nothing.
        Earlier notes said 5%; it is 6%.
  Tip   Few babies are conceived while the game is closed. Breed while you play.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

S4. Unborn babies already count toward the population
  What  A pregnant or nursing mother adds 1, 2 or 3 to the population number before her babies
        appear.
  Tip   Housing and population limits (below) can be reached "early". The Highest Population
        statistic can show more villagers than you can see.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code, re-traced] in all five games.

AGEING, SICKNESS AND DEATH

S5. Old age: a hidden death roll every birthday past 55, 63 or 71
  What  On each birthday past 55 (Medicine 1), 63 (Medicine 2) or 71 (Medicine 3), the villager
        dies with chance 10% times the number of years over that age. Death is certain by 65, 73
        or 81.
  Tip   Medicine is the lifespan technology. Each level adds 8 years.
  Patch A New Home: Magic Fruit of Life Alters Mortality (+7 years).
        The Secret City: Nature Level 3 Actually Alters Mortality (+7 years).
        Other games: not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] in all five games. [save] In The Lost Children the oldest villagers in your saves
        are 76 to 80 at Medicine 3, under the limit of 81.

S6. "Elderly" villagers get sick more and heal much more slowly
  What  From age 50 (Medicine 1), 58 (Medicine 2) or 66 (Medicine 3), a villager:
        - has an extra 1.5% chance of falling sick on every age step (about 26% per year), and
        - regains health only one fifth as often as younger villagers.
        This is why most villagers aged 50+ that you saw live were ill.
  Tip   Expect sickness among your elders and keep a healer busy. Medicine 3 moves "elderly" to 66.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] in all five games. [observed live] Villagers aged 50+ were mostly ill.

S7. A work injury hides in research
  What  2% of research sessions cost the researcher 0 to 9 health.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] in all five games.

S8. The Difficulty setting quietly changes food and health
  What  Easy, Normal (or Medium) and Hard change how much food villagers eat, how often they
        starve, sicken and heal, and the starting food. The exact numbers are in each game's
        section.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

BREEDING

S9. A pairing's success depends on the skill of the villager you dragged
  What  A manual drop makes two hidden rolls, both using the Parenting skill of the villager you
        DRAGGED (not the one you dropped it on):
        - "Do they get along?": a roll of 0-99 must be at most Parenting + 30 (A New Home) or
          Parenting + 33 (the other games). The other games change the +33 to +66 if the villager
          likes "learning" and +16 if they dislike it. The Tree of Life and New Believers add +15
          at Learning level 2 and +30 at Learning level 3.
        - Conception: a roll of 0-299, plus 50, minus 50 for each level of the fertility
          technology, must be at most that same Parenting skill. The fertility technology is
          Medicine, or "Fertility" in A New Home. A failed conception roll shows no message.
  Tip   Drag the partner with the higher Parenting. The fertility technology matters a lot:
        at level 1 the conception chance is (Parenting + 1) out of 300, at level 3 it is
        (Parenting + 101) out of 300.
  Patch Learning Skills Never Fails makes the get-along roll always succeed. The conception roll
        is not changed.
  Tag   [code, re-traced] in all five games.

S10. Your first pairing in a new village is guaranteed
  What  Until the tutorial message "Good! They get along..." has been shown in a village, a
        pairing skips the conception roll (and in most games the low-food refusal). The age check
        and the other checks still apply.
  Tip   This only happens once per village, so don't expect the same luck again.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] in all five games. See A New Home rule 7 for exactly when it is switched on.

S11. Twins and triplets are rare and need the top fertility technology
  What  The Lost Children, The Secret City, The Tree of Life and New Believers: only at Medicine
        level 3. Then 7% of conceptions are multiple, and a quarter of those are triplets. Overall
        that is 5.25% twins and 1.75% triplets. Below Medicine 3 every birth is single.
        A New Home works differently; see its rule 10.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher. The population modes only stop a litter from overflowing the
        village.
  Tag   [code]

S12. Babies take two years to arrive
  What  The baby becomes its own villager 40 age steps (2 game years) after conception. That is
        about 4 real hours at Normal.
  Tag   [code]

S13. In a tiny village the newborn's sex is not random
  What  When the village has 6 or fewer people (counting unborn babies), a newborn's sex is forced
        whenever one sex has 2 or fewer members. In A New Home and The Lost Children the code
        pushes toward the sex that is ALREADY in the majority, the opposite of balancing. That
        looks like a bug in the original game. The direction in the other three games was not
        settled.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] Not seen in a running game. The direction is [unverified] for The Secret City, The
        Tree of Life and New Believers.

JOBS

S14. Bodies get buried before most other work
  What  While an unburied body lies in the village, any idle adult goes to it 25% of the time, and
        a villager with Healing 50 or more does so about 77% of the time. A body left alone
        vanishes by itself after 240 age steps (about a day of real time at Normal).
        New Believers buries only after the Mausoleum is restored.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

A NEW HOME (VV1)
======================================================================================

BUILDING

1. A hut's scaffold appears long before builders will work on it
  What  Hut 9 is built at any population. Hut 10's scaffold appears at 15 villagers, but builders
        start on it only above 22. Hut 11's scaffold appears at 28 villagers, but builders start on
        it only above 45. Drawing a scaffold marks it as "started" (progress 1), but the stock
        builder code never looks at that mark.
  Tip   A bare scaffold at 15 to 22 villagers (or 28 to 45) is waiting for your population.
  Patch v1.35.44, Builder Action Fixes and Builders Fix Huts When Idle (both carry it):
        - builders start hut 10 or 11 only after you have worked on it (dropped a villager on it)
        - once started, builders finish it at any population, including during catch-up
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

2. Builders abandon a half-built hut if the population drops
  What  The population test is made on every decision. If the village falls back to 22 or fewer
        (hut 10) or 45 or fewer (hut 11), builders stop working on that hut, even halfway through,
        until the population rises again.
  Tip   A plague, starvation or an island event can freeze construction without any message.
  Patch v1.35.44 (as in rule 1): a started hut is finished at any population.
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

3. With 400 or more food, builders get fewer chances to build
  What  Below 400 food the game gives every villager an extra job attempt and a "carry on with
        what you were doing" step. At 400 or more it does not. A builder still gets one
        construction attempt per decision, but loses the second.
        This happens only while you play; catch-up has no food gate for builders.
  Tip   A well-fed village builds more slowly. This is not a bug in your village.
  Patch Builder Action Fixes and Builders Fix Huts When Idle give builders the attempt at 400+
        food about 3 times in 4.
  Tag   [code, re-traced] The previous note ("builders get no construction attempt at 400+") was
        too strong.

4. 21% of builder decisions skip huts, and below Construction level 3 huts are never fixed
  What  - Hut priority: on about 79% of decisions a builder goes to an unfinished, unlocked hut
          first. On the other 21% (a roll of 20 or less out of 100) it skips huts and looks at
          projects instead, unless it was already working on a hut.
        - The 21% skip on hut fixing: when it fires, the builder reports "started" but has
          nothing to do, so it stands idle.
        - Other projects (beach, blockage, field and so on) are only continued once someone has
          started them.
        - Below Construction level 3 the Building job gives up before it ever reaches hut fixing.
  Patch Builders Fix Huts When Idle (build first; idle builders fix huts at any Construction
        level). Builders and Healers Work First raises the odds further.
  Tag   [code]

BREEDING

5. A New Home has no age limit on a manual drop, but low food blocks most drops
  What  Both villagers must be 18 or older, of opposite sex, alive, not sick and not
        busy/pregnant. There is no upper age for either sex.
        Below 400 food a drop goes ahead only 26% of the time; the other 74% get "too hungry".
        When a drop fails because the village is at 90 or more, the game shows no message.
  Tip   Get food to 400+ before you breed by hand.
  Patch Birth Control adds a refusal for women aged 50+. Manual Drop-Breeding overrides Birth
        Control takes it back out for drops (it only matters when Birth Control is ticked).
  Tag   [code, re-traced] There is no 50+ refusal in stock A New Home.

6. Housing gates and the population limit
  What  To have a baby, the village needs:
        - hut 9 from 15 villagers
        - hut 10 from 25
        - hut 11 from 50
        90 is the hard limit. Unborn babies count (rule S4).
  Tip   Get hut 10 finished before 25 people and hut 11 before 50, or breeding stops.
  Patch Collection Progression Max Pop and Immediate Fixed Max Pop raise the limit to 256.
        No Population Increase keeps 90. The hut gates are not changed.
  Tag   [code]

7. The first pairing in a new village is guaranteed, unless you turned the help off
  What  A new village switches on the tutorial tip "Good! They get along...". While that tip is
        waiting to be shown, a drop skips both the low-food roll and the conception roll and
        always succeeds if the basic checks pass. The tip is then shown and switched off for
        good.
        If you answer No to "Would you like HELP the first time?", the tutorial tips are switched
        off, and this freebie with them.
        This is a real rule, not dead code. It runs once per village.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] Which button of the help question turns the tips off is inferred, not observed.

8. One embrace can make several women pregnant
  What  When a villager does the autonomous "Embracing" action, the game ignores the partner it
        walked to. It scans the whole village and gives every eligible adult of the opposite sex
        their own food roll (below 400 food), skill roll and conception roll. It does not stop
        after a success, so one man can father several babies at once. There is no family check.
  Tip   A single active father siring most of the village is normal in this game.
  Patch Birth Control (partners under 50 only; the Parenting preference must be ticked).
  Tag   [code]

9. Villagers choose Parenting on their own only if it beats their other skills by about 15
  What  When the job picker scores Parenting, it subtracts 15 from the skill. A ticked Parenting
        preference overrides this 86% of the time.
  Patch Birth Control.
  Tag   [code]

10. Twins need Fertility 3 or the Magic Fish; triplets need both
  What  Twins are possible only with Fertility level 3 OR the Magic Fish of Fertility puzzle
        solved: 7% of conceptions. Triplets need BOTH, and are then 25% of multiple births. At
        best that is 5.25% twins and 1.75% triplets.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

11. How a baby inherits talent depends on how it was conceived
  What  The baby starts with points in the best skill of one parent, chosen 50/50:
        - 5 to 7 points after your manual drop
        - 10 to 19 points after autonomous Embracing
        Parenting is never inherited. If Parenting is the chosen skill, the baby gets Farming 0
        instead.
  Tip   Let villagers pair on their own if you want talented babies.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] [save] Your birth log shows babies with 6 to 7 points in one skill and Breeding
        always 0.

JOBS

12. How the job picker really works
  What  - 15% of decisions produce no job at all.
        - Each skill is considered only 84% of the time.
        - A ticked preference overrides the pick 86% of the time.
        - The job then starts only on a roll of 0-99 below (skill + 40).
        A skill of 0 or 1 therefore never works on its own, even with the preference ticked.
  Tip   Don't tick a preference for a skill the villager does not have. Drag them to the job
        first.
  Patch Builders and Healers Work First and Builder Action Fixes raise the odds for building and
        healing.
  Tag   [code]

13. Dislikes quietly cancel work
  What  - A villager who dislikes "work" refuses 70% of job picks.
        - Disliking "medicine" skips Healing half the time.
        - Disliking swimming, the ocean, dancing and similar vetoes those activities.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

14. Beginners keep repeating whatever you last dragged them to
  What  If a villager's best skill is under 50 and all skills add up to under 70, the game makes
        them carry on with the task you last dragged them to. Except on Easy, they need at least
        1 point in that skill.
  Tip   One drag is enough to set a beginner working.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

15. Swimming, dancing, the garden and worship need 2,000 food
  What  These leisure and worship activities happen only at 2,000+ food. The worship chance is
        3 x Spirituality level, plus 3 for each shrine project completed.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

16. Work starts at 14
  What  Under 14 a villager only does child things. The game's own tip says "around 14".
  Tag   [code]

SKILLS, SCHOOL AND RESEARCH

17. Skill gain: fast at first, then very slow
  What  - A work task succeeds on a roll of 0-99 at most (skill + 30). A failed roll gains
          nothing.
        - Each success gives (50 - skill) / 5: +10 at 0, +5 at 25, +1 at 45.
        - From about 46 upward, a success gives +1 only with chance 26% x (100 - skill)%. That is
          2.6% at 90.
        - Titles: Trainee from 20, Adept from 50, Master from 90.
  Patch Learning Skills Never Fails (every roll succeeds). The gains are not changed.
  Tag   [code]

18. School gives no skill
  What  The stock "Going to school" lesson awards nothing.
  Patch School Lessons Grant Skill (7 to 9 points in one random skill). School Lessons Stop at 50.
  Tag   [repo research]

19. Research pays by Science level and stops at the top
  What  A research session pays Research skill / 7, / 5 or / 3 at Science level 1, 2 or 3, so
        Research under 7 at Science 1 earns nothing. Once all six technologies are at level 3,
        researchers stop.
  Patch Continue Research at Max Technologies.
  Tag   [code]

FOOD AND HEALTH

20. Berries shrink as the bush empties
  What  - Each forage gives (berries left / 130) berries, at least 2.
        - The bush starts at 1,400 (1,900 on Easy) and regrows by 30 every 4 real hours at
          Normal.
        - Crops reset to +800 on the same clock once Farming level is 2 or more.
        - A crop harvest gives 7 food.
        - At Farming level 1, an empty bush leaves farmers "busy" doing nothing.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

21. Eating and starving
  What  - Each villager eats 2 food per age step.
        - At 200 food or less, half the time they skip the meal and lose 1 health instead.
        - Above 2,000 food (Normal), at any food level (Hard), never (Easy): they eat 2 extra with
          76% chance.
        - At 0 food they lose 1 health 76% of the time (always on Hard).
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

22. Children never fall ill, except from Measles
  What  Only villagers aged 14+ can catch a random illness. Per age step the chance is 4 in 2,400
        (Medicine 1), 4 in 4,800 (Medicine 2) or 3 in 10,000 (Medicine 3), plus the elderly risk
        in S6. On Easy the check runs only half the time. A sick villager loses 1 health 71% of
        the time (about 22% at health 20 or less).
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

23. Healers never cure children on their own
  What  A healer's "Healing someone" action rolls the healer's Healing skill once for every
        villager and cures each sick one that passes. It also trains Healing on every roll, so it
        is fast in a big village. But it cures only villagers aged 14+, so children with Measles
        are never cured autonomously. Catch-up cures are adults-only too.
        Dropping a healer on a child does cure them. When all four herbs are understood, a
        dropped cure also restores 10 to 29 health.
  Tip   During Measles, drop your healer on each sick child yourself.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] Where the drag-cure's own success roll happens was not found [unverified].

24. Natural healing
  What  A fed, healthy villager gains +1 health per age step: always on Easy, half the time
        otherwise, and one fifth as often once elderly (S6).
  Tag   [code]

ISLAND EVENTS AND CATCH-UP

25. Island events: timing
  What  - The first event comes 30 minutes into a new village, then one every 12 to 23 real
          hours.
        - Paused time does not count, and an event waits until its chosen villager has caught
          up, so events do not pile up while you are away.
        - After the first event (always a choice event): 20% are choice events, 40% passive
          events and 40% a Mysterious Crate.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code] That no events happen during catch-up is inferred.

26. Island events: hidden conditions and sizes
  What  - Trapped Whale needs Spirituality below 3.
        - The Visitor needs Medicine below 3.
        - Locusts need Farming level 2+.
        - Big Wave needs the beach cleaned.
        - Measles needs a child, and makes every child sick.
        - Barrel O' Babies needs room under the limit. It brings 1 baby under 15 villagers, 2 at
          15 to 23 and 3 at 24+.
        - An event's size is population / 3, between 1 and 10.
        - The Bad Little Monkey takes size x 900 tech points.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher (automatic capacity safety stops overflow).
  Tag   [code]

27. A New Home keeps plant study going during catch-up
  What  Catch-up calls the Building and Healing jobs directly, so a healer with nobody to treat
        carries on studying the plant. The Lost Children does not (see its rule 30).
  Patch Healers Study Plants Regardless of Food (for live play at 400+ food; catch-up already
        continued).
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

THE LOST CHILDREN (VV2)
======================================================================================

TIME

1. "1/2 speed" is really 0.6 speed
  What  Normal is 6 real minutes per age step, 2x is 3 minutes, and "1/2 speed" is 10 minutes
        (not 12).
  Tag   [code] [save]

BUILDING

2. Builders never start the 2nd or 3rd population hut by themselves
  What  The scaffold of the second population hut appears at 21 villagers, the third at 46.
        Each scaffold shows progress 1. Builders work on a hut only when the population is above
        22 (or above 45) AND its progress is at least 2, which only your drop can give it. They
        drop it again whenever the population falls back to 22 (or 45).
  Tip   Drop a builder on each new hut once to start it.
  Patch v1.35.44, Builders Fix Huts When Idle: builders start a hut you have started, then finish
        it at any population, including during catch-up.
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

3. Each construction step is skipped 21% of the time
  What  Construction goes through a fixed order (own task, project 1, the huts, then projects
        unlocked by Engineering levels 2 and 3). Each step goes ahead only on a roll above 20 out
        of 100 (79%). Below Engineering
        level 3 the Building job gives up before hut fixing.
  Patch Builders Fix Huts When Idle and Builders and Healers Work First.
  Tag   [code]

4. The Hospital needs Medicine 3 and Engineering 3, and your start
  What  Its scaffold appears only once both levels are 3. Builders work on it only after you have
        started it.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

BREEDING

5. Housing gates and the population limit
  What  To have a baby the village needs:
        - 1 hut from 15 villagers
        - 2 huts from 25
        - 3 huts from 50
        The limit is 90, plus 5 per completed collection (25 when all four are done), so 115.
  Patch Collection Progression Max Pop and Immediate Fixed Max Pop raise the limit. No Population
        Increase keeps 115. The hut gates are not changed.
  Tag   [code] [save] Peak population of 90 to 91.

6. Manual drop: a woman aged 50+ is refused silently, after the rolls
  What  Both villagers must be 18+, of opposite sex, healthy, not sick and not nursing. Below 300
        food there is a 34% "too hungry" refusal. Then come the two rolls (S9). Only after the
        conception roll has succeeded is a woman aged 50+ refused, with no message. The
        "elderly and not interested" message appears only when an earlier check failed.
  Patch Manual Drop-Breeding overrides Birth Control (works with or without Birth Control).
  Tag   [code, re-traced]

7. Autonomous pairing: women under 50 only, and one man can father several babies
  What  An embracing villager scans the whole village twice. Every eligible opposite-sex villager
        gets their own food check (34% veto below 300 food), skill roll and conception roll, so
        one man can make several women pregnant at once. The woman must be under 50; men have no
        age limit.
  Patch Birth Control.
  Tag   [code]

8. How a baby inherits talent depends on how it was conceived
  What  The baby gets the best skill of one parent, chosen 50/50:
        - 10 to 19 points from autonomous or catch-up pairing
        - only 5 to 7 points from your drop
        On a drop where both parents share the same best skill, Culture changes it to 4 to 5
        (level 1), 5 to 8 (level 2) or 7 to 11 (level 3). Parenting is never inherited.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

9. Newborns start at 90 health, and most have no likes
  What  A newborn starts at 90 health. 70% have no likes, 21% one and 9% two. Dislikes follow the
        same odds.
  Tag   [code]

10. Every pregnancy trains the mother
  What  The mother gains max(5, (100 - Parenting) / 10) Parenting, up to 100.
  Tag   [code]

JOBS

11. Two decisions in three do nothing
  What  The idle scheduler does nothing on 66% of its runs.
  Tip   Idle-looking villagers are normal.
  Tag   [code]

12. The job picker's hidden rules
  What  - Unless the villager likes "work", 15% of picks give no job. Disliking "work" adds a
          further 70%.
        - Each skill is considered 84% of the time, and Parenting scores 15 lower.
        - A ticked preference wins 86% of the time.
        - The job starts only if the skill is above 5, and then with chance (skill + 40)%.
        A preference on a skill of 5 or less leaves the villager idle.
  Patch Builders and Healers Work First (builders and healers only).
  Tag   [code]

13. The 300-food line
  What  Below 300 food every villager gets an extra job attempt, and healers carry on studying
        plants. At 300+ they lose that attempt, and healers stop plant study.
  Patch Healers Study Plants Regardless of Food. Builders Fix Huts When Idle and Builders and
        Healers Work First (for builders).
  Tag   [code]

14. Plenty of food makes villagers quit long jobs
  What  At 4,000+ food, a villager who doesn't like "work" keeps at a multi-step job only about
        half the time, and one who dislikes work only about a third.
  Patch Not changed by the patcher.
  Tag   [code]

15. Dislikes cancel jobs

======================================================================================
LOOKS LIKE A RULE BUT NEVER RUNS
======================================================================================
These checks are in the stock code but can never take effect in the shipping game.

- The "village under 7" limit on breeding and curing (A New Home, The Lost Children, The Secret
  City, The Tree of Life)
  The drop handler and the catch-up cure actions say "allowed if a flag is set, otherwise only
  when the population is under 7". It looks like a leftover from the old trial version. The flag
  is always 1, so the population test never matters.
  - In A New Home the flag lives in the game's global settings file (Virtual Villagers0.ldw). The
    game sets it to 1 when it creates that file on the very first launch, and nothing ever sets
    it back. Every Virtual Villagers0.ldw in your folders holds 1 [save].
  - The other three games set it to 1 at every start-up.
  What it guards is the normal pairing and the normal catch-up cure. It is not a special cure
  and not a small-tribe feature. Neither New Believers research pass reported this check.

- The job picker's "25% chance to breed without the Parenting preference"
  This is your settled decision: dead code. In The Tree of Life and New Believers it is provably
  unreachable, because Parenting is only offered when the preference is ticked.

- The job picker's "low food" argument (A New Home, The Lost Children)
  It is passed in but never read. The only real food difference is the extra job attempt
  (A New Home rule 3, The Lost Children rule 13).

- "Good" and "bad" island-event lists (A New Home, The Lost Children)
  The routine that picks them always returns 0, so only the mixed list ever runs.

- Two A New Home events that can never happen
  "The Furry Food" and "A Mighty Storm" (food set to 0) always send the game back to choose a
  different event.

- Level-0 technology branches (all games)
  Medicine, Science and the other starting technologies begin at level 1, so the code paths for
  level 0 (for example a 47-year death age) never run.

- The Secret City
  - The ageing code reads Medicine and Nature and discards them (see its rule 25).
  - A catch-up dice roll is drawn and ignored.
  - A dormant totem selector is never set.

- New Believers
  - Building options 5 and 10 (refusals for disliking plants or lifting) are never offered.
  - A catch-up dice roll is drawn and ignored.
  - The event scheduler's 20% branch gives the same delay either way.


======================================================================================
NOTES FOR THE FUN PATCHER (things this research found in the patches' own text)
======================================================================================
1. Manual Drop-Breeding overrides Birth Control: The Tree of Life, New Believers, probably The
   Secret City
   The patched 50+ check sits in the routine that live autonomous embraces also use. Traced: the
   "Embracing" state handler queues the action that calls the drop routine. So the patch most
   likely lifts the 50+ refusal for villagers pairing on their own too, while its description
   says "Only the manual drop is changed". Not tested in a running game.

2. Builders Fix Huts When Idle in A New Home
   The 400-food limit it bypasses applies only while you play. Catch-up never had it. The
   descriptions already say "The food bypass acts only while you play".
