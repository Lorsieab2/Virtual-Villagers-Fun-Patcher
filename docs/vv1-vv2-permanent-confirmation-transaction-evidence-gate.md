# VV1/VV2 permanent confirmation transaction evidence gate

Status: **STOP**. This additive contract does not enable, publish, register, build, or emit any Origins route. Historical command indices and prices are pinned exactly; all native ABI, complete-folder, and runtime/fullscreen receipt fields are intentionally null or empty.

The covered permanent Buy-only routes and historical prices are Time Warp (50,000), Island Event (30,000), Barrel of Babies (75,000), legacy Cure (30,000), Grant Youth (50,000), selected Full Mastery (100,000), selected Running (40,000), and Set Age to 18 (50,000). Food and Tech Doublers are excluded because their historical ownership model permits Remove. The legacy Cure row is covered only so its sickness-only/precharge/result bytes cannot be misrepresented as Full Heal or safe transaction evidence.

A future route must prove a complete dry run before confirmation; valid same-process owner HWND and IDOK-only confirmation; fresh world, selected index, record, account, eligibility, prestate, and funds after confirmation; an exact native setter ABI with side effects and readback; complete postverification before exactly one native deduction; final account readback; fullscreen leave/restore/cleanup; and natural zero/one/many result text. Cancel and no-op need proved no-charge. Partial effects must disclose that native changes may remain and rollback is not claimed. If final charge cannot be proved, wording must say charge is unknown and must not claim that no points were deducted.

Existing precharge, direct record/account stores, withdrawn diagnostic payloads, and legacy result helpers are rejection fixtures only. Unknown or synthetic evidence fails closed. Runtime/player/native status remains STOP.
