CREDIT POLICY
* "Original concept" identifies who first supplied the premise, mechanism,
  character direction, or material for an event; it does NOT necessarily
  mean that person drafted the prose.
* "Original event text" identifies who wrote the earliest event manuscript.
* "Additional writing" identifies new dialogue, branches, outcomes and
  event text written by the named contributor.
* "Substantial revision/expansion" identifies a major later rewrite or
  extension, without transferring ownership of the original work.
* "Creative direction" credits requested themes, constraints, and outcomes.
* Editorial formatting and small grammatical fixes do not count as
  original authorship or a substantial rewrite.
* Uncertain provenance is not silently attributed to either contributor.

Lorsieab2 originated the ongoing collection's worldbuilding requirements,
selection of games, preferred emotional and comedic range, faction and
protected-character constraints, and numerous event premises and outcome
mechanics. ChatGPT significantly expanded the event collection through
new story prose, branches, randomized dialogue, reputation systems,
continuity notes and integration guidance. These are distinct credits:
"Concept / creative direction: Lorsieab2" and "Event writing and
substantial expansion: ChatGPT" are both appropriate for many events.

supplied event manuscripts and the cumulative v11 collection. It does
not claim that every paragraph has been compared against every historical
revision. Where a specific sentence's provenance is uncertain, the

ChatGPT receives credit only for identifiable additions or substantial
expansions. The separately uploaded "More Island Events" manuscript
and "Aspiring Adventurer" manuscript remain Lorsieab2-authored source
materials; they are not silently incorporated as ChatGPT writing.
