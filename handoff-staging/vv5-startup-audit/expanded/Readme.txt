Welcome to Virtual Villagers: New Believers (v 1.00)

A message from the developer:

"First of all, thanks for the continued support and interest in the Virtual Villagers series! We are constantly experimenting with ways to keep each new chapter fresh and interesting and worthy of all the love. This time we have made some pretty interesting changes, as you are about to see. This time, it is all about YOU. The villagers, your beloved inhabitants of Isola, believe in you, and it shows. But there are others who do not share this perspective and will refuse your influence until you convince them otherwise. We hope you enjoy playing with your new powers and overcoming these new limitations. There are still more secrets of Isola to uncover, which will reveal more and more about the island and its story as we go forward. Enjoy!!"

Arthur Humphrey
Lead Designer
